# POL-002 – Data classification

## Levels
- **Public** – can be shared outside the bank (e.g. product brochures).
- **Internal** – for all staff; not for customers or third parties.
- **Confidential** – limited to staff with a business need (e.g. security incidents, DR design, vendor contracts).
- **Restricted** – highest level; named people only (e.g. executive pay, privileged keys).

## Rules
- Every document must have a classification label.
- Confidential and restricted data must not be sent to external AI services or personal email.
