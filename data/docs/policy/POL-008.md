# POL-008 – Privileged access and key management

## Privileged access
- Production admin accounts are kept in the PAM vault; passwords rotate after each use.
- Sessions are recorded and reviewed monthly.

## Keys
- HSM master keys are split between three key custodians; two are needed to load a key.
- Key ceremonies are recorded and witnessed by internal audit.
