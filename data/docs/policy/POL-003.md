# POL-003 – Acceptable use

## Summary
Bank IT equipment, email and internet access are for business use. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect work or security.

## Not allowed
- Installing unapproved software.
- Sharing passwords or MFA codes with anyone, including IT staff.
- Using bank email for personal sign-ups.
