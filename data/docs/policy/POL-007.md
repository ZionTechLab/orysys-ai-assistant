# POL-007 – Executive compensation framework 2026

## Scope
Corporate management and above.

## Structure
- Fixed pay benchmarked at the 75th percentile of local banks.
- Annual bonus up to 40% of fixed pay, based on bank profit (60%) and personal goals (40%).
- 30% of bonus deferred for 3 years.

## Approval
Board remuneration committee only.
