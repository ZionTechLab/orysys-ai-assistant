# POL-005 – Incident management

## Rules
- Severity follows RB-004.
- Customer-facing payment outages over 60 minutes are reported to the regulator within 24 hours.
- Repeat incidents (same root cause within 6 months) are escalated to the IT steering committee.
- Every SEV1 and SEV2 needs a post-incident review.
