# MTG-003 – Architecture board (Aug 2026)

## Topic
Repeated PayBridge-related outages (INC-2026-021, INC-2026-062).

## Decisions
- Approve a shared resilient client for all third-party gateways: 5-second timeout, circuit breaker, bulkhead thread pool, limited retries with backoff.
- All services calling PayBridge must use the shared client by end of Q3 2026.
- Payments team to keep an inventory of third-party consumers.

## Risks raised
- Some older services (e.g. wallet top-up) may still use the legacy client.
