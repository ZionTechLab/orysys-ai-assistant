# MTG-004 – PayBridge contract review

## Discussion
- PayBridge availability this year: 99.2% against a contracted 99.9%.
- Three degradations affected our customers (Mar, Jul, Oct expected).
- Service credits of LKR 4.5 million claimed for the March outage.

## Decisions
- Request a maintenance notification process with 7 days notice.
- Start evaluation of a second interbank gateway for redundancy in 2027.
