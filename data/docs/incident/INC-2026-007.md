# INC-2026-007 – Month-end salary payment delays (connection pool exhaustion)

## Summary
On 30 Jan 2026, the payment hub could not process corporate salary bulk files for 1 hour 50 minutes. The application ran out of database connections during the month-end peak.

## Impact
- 1,240 corporate salary files delayed
- ~96,000 employee salary credits late by up to 2 hours
- Corporate relationship managers received many complaints

## Root Cause
The payment hub connection pool was fixed at 50 connections. Month-end volume was 4x a normal day. Long-running reconciliation queries held connections, so new payment requests waited and timed out.

## Resolution
The pool was increased to 120 and reconciliation jobs were paused until the salary run completed.

## Actions
- Load test the payment hub at 5x normal volume before month-end.
- Move reconciliation queries to the read replica.
- See RB-002 Connection pool tuning.
