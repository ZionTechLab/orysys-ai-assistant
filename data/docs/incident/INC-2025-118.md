# INC-2025-118 – Card authorisation failures (TLS certificate expiry)

## Summary
On 2 Dec 2025 at 00:00, the TLS certificate on the card switch connection to the international card network expired. All POS and e-commerce card authorisations were declined for 47 minutes.

## Impact
- Duration: 47 minutes (00:00 – 00:47)
- ~22,400 declined card transactions
- Customer complaints: 310; social media escalation

## Root Cause
The certificate was tracked in a spreadsheet owned by a team member who had left the bank. No automated expiry alert existed for the card switch certificates.

## Resolution
Payments engineering issued and installed a new certificate using the emergency change process (RB-001).

## Actions
- Move all certificates to a central inventory with 30/14/7-day expiry alerts.
- Add the card switch to the certificate scanner.
