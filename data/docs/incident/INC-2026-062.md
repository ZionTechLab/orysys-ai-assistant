# INC-2026-062 – PayBridge latency cascade

## Summary
On 22 Jul 2026, PayBridge latency increased to 20 seconds during their own maintenance. Our timeout had been reduced to 5 seconds, but the circuit breaker was still not in production. Retries tripled the load and bill payments failed for 40 minutes.

## Impact
- ~11,500 failed bill payments and interbank transfers
- No double debits (idempotency keys were live)

## Root Cause
Automatic retries (3 attempts) without a circuit breaker multiplied traffic to an already slow gateway. The PayBridge maintenance notice was sent to a shared mailbox nobody monitored.

## Resolution
Retries disabled manually; traffic normalised after PayBridge maintenance ended.

## Actions
- Deliver the circuit breaker (approved at architecture board MTG-003).
- Route third-party maintenance notices to the payments on-call channel.
