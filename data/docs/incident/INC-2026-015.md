# INC-2026-015 – HR portal unavailable

## Summary
The HR self-service portal was unavailable for 3 hours on 20 Feb 2026, the last day of appraisal submissions.

## Root Cause
The document storage volume reached 100% because uploaded attachments were never archived.

## Resolution
Old attachments were archived and the volume was extended. The appraisal deadline was extended by two days.

## Actions
- Add disk usage alerts at 80% and 90%.
- Monthly archive job for attachments older than 12 months.
