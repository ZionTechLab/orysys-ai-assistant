# INC-2026-088 – Phishing campaign against branch staff

## Summary
A phishing email impersonating the IT service desk was sent to 640 branch staff. 23 staff entered their credentials on a fake page.

## Impact
- 23 accounts reset; no evidence of fraudulent transactions
- Two accounts were used to send internal phishing emails before being blocked

## Root Cause
MFA was not yet enforced for webmail access from outside the bank network.

## Resolution
Accounts reset, sessions revoked, fake domain taken down.

## Actions
- Enforce MFA for all external webmail access.
- Run phishing awareness training for branch staff.
