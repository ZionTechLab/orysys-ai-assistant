# INC-2026-049 – Merchant card payments declined (certificate expiry)

## Summary
On 11 Jun 2026, the TLS client certificate used by the merchant acquiring gateway expired. Card payments at merchants using our POS terminals were declined for 38 minutes.

## Impact
- ~9,800 declined merchant transactions
- 3 large retail merchants escalated to their relationship managers

## Root Cause
The acquiring gateway certificate was not added to the central certificate inventory created after INC-2025-118. Only card switch certificates had been migrated.

## Resolution
Certificate renewed using RB-001.

## Actions
- Scan all network endpoints to discover every certificate, not only known ones.
- Automate renewal where possible.
