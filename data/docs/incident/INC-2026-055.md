# INC-2026-055 – End-of-day batch overrun

## Summary
The core banking end-of-day (EOD) batch on 30 Jun 2026 (half-year closing) finished at 08:40 instead of 05:00. Branches opened with balances not updated.

## Root Cause
Half-year interest capitalisation ran on all savings accounts in a single thread.

## Resolution
Branches were asked to use offline balance checks until 08:40.

## Actions
- Parallelise interest capitalisation by branch.
- See RB-005 EOD batch operations.
