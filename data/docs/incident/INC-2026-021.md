# INC-2026-021 – Interbank transfer failures (PayBridge timeouts)

## Summary
On 14 Mar 2026, the external PayBridge interbank gateway became slow (responses over 30 seconds). Our payment hub kept waiting for every request, and all worker threads were blocked. Interbank transfers and bill payments failed for 1 hour 15 minutes.

## Impact
- ~31,000 failed or timed-out transfers
- Mobile and internet banking showed generic errors
- Duplicate transfer risk: 42 customers retried and were debited twice (reversed next day)

## Root Cause
The PayBridge client had a 60-second timeout and no circuit breaker. When PayBridge degraded, threads piled up and the whole payment hub stopped responding, including internal transfers that do not use PayBridge.

## Resolution
PayBridge recovered at 11:40. The payment hub was restarted to clear blocked threads.

## Actions
- Reduce timeout to 5 seconds.
- Add a circuit breaker and a separate thread pool (bulkhead) for PayBridge calls.
- Add idempotency keys to prevent double debits.
- See RB-003.
