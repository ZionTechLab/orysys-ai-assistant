# INC-2025-101 – Internet banking login failures (DNS misconfiguration)

## Summary
On 9 Oct 2025 between 08:12 and 09:05, about 35% of internet banking logins failed. Payments were not affected once customers were logged in.

## Impact
- Duration: 53 minutes
- ~18,000 failed login attempts
- No financial loss

## Root Cause
A DNS change made during a planned network change pointed the login API record at a decommissioned load balancer. The change was not peer reviewed.

## Resolution
DNS record reverted at 09:01. Caches cleared by 09:05.

## Actions
- Enforce peer review for DNS changes (POL-004 Change Management).
- Add synthetic login monitoring every minute.
