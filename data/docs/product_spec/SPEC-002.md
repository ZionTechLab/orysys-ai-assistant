# SPEC-002 – Virtual debit cards

## Goal
Customers create a virtual debit card in the app for online shopping, with its own limit.

## Requirements
- Card issued instantly; can be frozen or deleted in the app.
- Optional single-use card number for one purchase.
- 3-D Secure required for all online transactions.
