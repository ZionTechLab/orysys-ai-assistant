# RB-005 – End-of-day batch operations

## Schedule
EOD starts at 22:30 and must complete by 05:00.

## Monitoring
- Check batch progress every hour on the batch console.
- If any job is 30 minutes behind, call the core banking on-call.

## Month-end and half-year
- Interest capitalisation runs in parallel by branch group (after INC-2026-055).
- Start EOD 30 minutes earlier on half-year and year-end days.

## If EOD overruns past 07:00
- Inform branch operations to use offline balance checks.
- Do not start online services until account balances are updated.
