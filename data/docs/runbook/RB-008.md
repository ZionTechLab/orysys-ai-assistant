# RB-008 – Disaster recovery failover (payment hub)

## Targets
- RTO: 2 hours, RPO: 5 minutes.

## Decision
Failover is approved by the CIO or the Head of IT Operations only.

## Steps
1. Stop inbound traffic at the load balancer.
2. Confirm database replication lag is under 5 minutes.
3. Promote the DR database and start payment hub nodes in the DR site.
4. Switch DNS for the payment APIs to the DR site.
5. Run smoke tests: internal transfer, interbank transfer, card authorisation.
