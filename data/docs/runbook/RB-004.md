# RB-004 – Incident severity and escalation

## Severity levels
- **SEV1** – customer payments or card services down for many customers. Bridge within 10 minutes. CIO informed within 30 minutes.
- **SEV2** – partial outage or a single channel down. Bridge within 30 minutes.
- **SEV3** – internal system or minor impact. Handled in business hours.

## Escalation
1. On-call engineer → team lead → duty manager → Head of IT Operations → CIO.
2. Payment outages above 60 minutes must be reported to the regulator by the compliance team.

## After the incident
- Incident report within 2 working days.
- Post-incident review within 10 working days; actions tracked by the change board.
