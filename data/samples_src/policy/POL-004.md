# POL-004 – Change management

## Change types
- **Standard** – pre-approved, low risk.
- **Normal** – approved by the change advisory board (CAB) weekly.
- **Emergency** – during an incident; approved by the duty manager, reviewed by CAB later.

## Rules
- All production changes need peer review, including DNS and firewall changes.
- Change freeze from 2 days before month-end to 1 day after for payment systems.
- Post-incident actions are tracked as changes with an owner and due date.
